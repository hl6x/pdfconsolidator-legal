# Privacy Policy for PDF Consolidator
**Last Updated: May 2026**

PDF Consolidator is committed to protecting your privacy. 
This policy explains how we handle your data with a strict offline-first approach.

### 1. 100% Offline Processing<
* All document merging and editing happens entirely on your device. 
* No files or document contents are ever uploaded to any servers.
* No internet connection is required for core document features.
* Your sensitive documents remain strictly under your control at all times.

### 2. Permissions
* The app requires the following permissions to function:
* We only request permissions absolutely necessary for the app to function:
* Storage access: To allow you to select, edit, and save your PDF files.
* No background tracking, location harvesting, or data mining.

### 3. Third-Party Services
* This app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries.

### 4. Data Security
Since your data never leaves your device, you are responsible for maintaining backups of your important files. 
Temporary files are cleared automatically.
